## Goal
Breaking the Sorting Gate no longer ends the run: the crew rolls into a second region, the Slag
Flats, with its own boards, a new enemy kind, tougher squads and its own gate boss, The Pour.
Winning THAT wins the run (until Act 3 exists).

## Scope
- In:
  - **Acts in the run**: `RunState.act`; `run.json` `acts` (a list of per-act overrides of the
    region, enemies, kinds, terrain and front rules, plus what arriving gives). Winning a gate
    that is not the last act's builds the next region -- same crew, hold, scrap and levels; the
    Reclaimer starts again behind. One action list still replays the whole run.
  - **The Slag Flats' boards**: three new maps used only in Act 2 (slag lakes, pipe runs), and
    slag scattered on generated boards there. Maps name the acts they belong to (`acts`).
  - **Yard sentinels**: a new enemy kind, `sentinel` -- plated (takes 1 less from every hit)
    and anchored (cannot be shoved). Act 2 squads are bigger, better equipped, more often special.
  - **The Pour**: Act 2's gate and keeper. Every other round it marks the hexes the crew stands
    on; a round later those hexes flood with slag for the rest of the fight. Telegraphed; the
    answer is to keep moving and to kill it before the floor runs out.
  - The map says which act it is; the result screen says the run is won only when it is.
- Out (deliberately): Act 3 (the Crucible, The Core); new models for Act 2's sites; new music.

## Steps
1. Acts in `RunSim` / `RunState`; per-act rules; tests.
2. Flooding in `CombatSim` (state, clone, events); the `sentinel` and `pour` kinds; tests.
3. Data: `acts`, three maps, the gate. The view: pour marks, flooded hexes, act title.
4. Run bot through both acts; tune; suites; docs.

## Result

- **Acts**: `RunState.act`; `RunSim.rules_of(state, setup)` lays `run.json` `acts[act-1]` over the
  rules one level deep; `_next_act` on a broken gate that is not the last (new region, same crew,
  hold, scrap and levels, `arrive` repair and scrap, the front behind again); every per-site roll
  takes the act into its salt, Act 1's unchanged. verify_run 138 passed (127 + 11): Act 1 is
  generated exactly as before acts existed.
- **The Slag Flats**: `slag_lake`, `pipe_forest`, `cooling_flats` (`"acts": [2]`), slag scattered
  on generated boards; `slag_pit` and `slag_channel` serve both acts. The map titles the act.
- **Sentinels** (`plate` 1, `anchored`) and **The Pour** (`floods` 3, `every` 2, `hazard` 2:
  `CombatSim._pours`, `state.pour_marks` / `state.flooded`, both cloned; `POUR_MARKED`, `FLOODED`).
  verify_combat 207 passed (189 + 18, the four new boards bot-fought).
- **Balance** (run bot, 60 two-act runs each; Act 1 alone was 88.7% over 150):

| Act 2 dials | Won |
|---|---|
| bigger squads, more kinds | 95.0% |
| + 3 HP on every enemy, 4 gate escorts | 95.0% |
| + 1 damage on every enemy (**kept**) | **85.0%** |

  Extra HP alone does nothing to a crew with five or six levels; damage does. Losses with the
  kept dials fall across Act 2's columns (one at its gate).

## Decisions, lessons, open questions
- **A run is two acts until Act 3 exists**; winning the last act's gate wins the run.
- **An act is a rules overlay**, not code: squads, kinds, terrain, boards and the gate by data.
- Lesson: against a levelled crew enemy HP is not difficulty; enemy damage is.
- Open: is The Pour a fight worth having (the user plays it)? Is 85% over two acts right?
