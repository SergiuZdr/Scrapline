# Iteration 025 — Act 3: the Crucible

**Status:** in progress
**Started:** 2026-09-30 · **Finished:** —
**Answers:** play-test 7, PT7-1: "do act 3". Story and enemies as planned in `plans/story.md`
(the Crucible, the furnace-city; its keeper the Core) and `plans/enemies-and-ai.md` (the
Foundry Mind: coordinated, combo attacks, fields that change the terrain).

## Goal
Breaking The Pour no longer wins the run: the crew goes into the Crucible, a third region with
its own boards, ground that fights back, an enemy that makes its neighbours hit harder, and the
Core at its heart. Breaking the Core wins the run.

## Scope
- In:
  - **Act 3 in `run.json` `acts`**: the Crucible's region, squads (bigger, rarer parts, harder
    hits), kinds and terrain; arriving repairs and pays. Nothing in the run code is act-specific.
  - **Furnace flues** (a tile, `f`; not "vents", which is what heat does): every other round a flue blows -- 3 damage to whatever
    stands on it -- and the round before, the board says so. Scattered on the Crucible's boards.
  - **Conduits** (an enemy kind, the Foundry Mind's): every ally next to one hits 1 harder. The
    board draws the link; kill it, shove it away, or split them up.
  - **The Core** (the gate, `the_core.json`): bolted to its hex, it never moves. Every third
    round it marks every hex within 2 of it and a round later pulses: 4 damage to each of the
    crew standing there. It builds drones from a pad. Flues ring its chamber.
  - **The volley totals (024) include the round's start**: slag, floods, flues and pulses, so
    the number on a hex is what END TURN will do to it.
  - Three new boards, the story's act 3 lines and the run's real ending, glossary entries.
- Out (deliberately): new models (the Core is a big machine from existing parts, like the
  Sorter); music; new site kinds.

## Steps
1. Sim: flues, conduits, the Core's pulse (state, clone, events); `incoming` with the round's
   start; the AI stays off what blows next; tests.
2. Data: the tile, the kinds, four boards, the act, story and glossary.
3. The view: flues and their warning, pulse rings, conduit links, the Core's size, labels.
4. Run bot over three acts; tune; suites; docs.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] verify_combat: a flue blows on its rounds and not between; a conduit's neighbour hits 1
  harder, and not once it is gone; the Core never moves, marks its ring a round ahead and pulses

- [ ] verify_run: winning Act 2's gate starts Act 3; winning Act 3's gate wins the run; Acts 1
  and 2 are generated exactly as before.
- [ ] run_bot 150 through three acts: 0 illegal actions; the win rate recorded, Act 3 harder

- [ ] Screenshots of the Core's fight and an Act 3 board; every suite passes.
