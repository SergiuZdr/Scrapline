## What worked
> "At least currently I saw some opportunities to do good damage using the terrain —
> using the brain makes the battles a bit easier because of terrain and good
> attack/ability combination."

Terrain and ability combinations are the first thing the user has praised. 006's direction
is right. The problems above are about clarity, presentation and trust, not the design.

## Diagnosis
- **PT2-10 explains PT2-5 and PT2-7.** The hex tiles were drawn turned 30°, so tiles
  touched at their corners and the picture did not match which hexes are really
  neighbours. Distances counted by eye were wrong by one. The grapple case also has a real
  rule behind it: anchor frames cannot be moved, and nothing on screen said so.
- PT2-1 was scheduled for Iteration 008 and never said so to the user. **Reported problems
  are fixed before new features**, and anything deferred is said out loud.
