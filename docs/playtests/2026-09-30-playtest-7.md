## What the answers were

- **PT7-2**: the green mark is the **scrap carrier** mark (play-test 4): that enemy drops a
  scrap pile when destroyed; the ones without it drop nothing. The "weird spot": the label
  declutter (016) pushed a crowded machine's labels DOWN in world height, onto its own body,
  and the mark floated beside a tag it no longer lined up with.
- **PT7-4** was measured before anything was changed (`tools/measure_hitches.gd`, a bot fight):
  frames of 300–850 ms at nearly every attack and hit, up to 2.3 s at a torn arm. A stack
  sample showed the time in the GL driver compiling shaders. Two causes: Godot frees a
  StandardMaterial3D's shader with the last material using it, so every tracer, flash and
  marker built and threw away its material and compiled it again on the next shot (a
  transparent emissive one recompiled even when kept); and the drum's fireball lit an omni
  light the toon ramp ignores, which asked every lit material in range for a new variant.
- **PT7-7**: a beam flies along the straight line from the shooter through the hex AIMED AT.
  Off the six hex axes "right behind" is two hexes, and the line takes one of them; the beam
  also ends 2 hexes past the weapon's range (at half damage). The drum behind was on the
  other hex. Pierce 2 does go through two things and hit a third.
