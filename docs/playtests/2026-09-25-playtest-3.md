## Diagnosis
- **PT3-6 was intended**: a charge was designed to BE the move (`usable` refuses it once
  the machine has moved). The user is right that it is weak: a move plus an attack
  reaches as far and costs no cooldown.
- **PT3-7 is a bug**: the arc only jumps unit to unit. It never reaches a barrel or crate,
  and never starts from a prop that was hit.
- **PT3-8 is a rule**: a pile is collected only on the hex where a move ENDS. Nothing on
  the board says so, so it reads as broken.
- **Found while checking PT3-7:** `PartText` still printed every shot weapon as "lob",
  a leftover from 002's "line" shape. The Rail Lance read "lob 1-3" on every card.
- **PT3-9**: scrap buys only repairs, rebuilds and hold room. With HP carrying well, a
  good run never needs the first two, so scrap piles up with nothing to buy.
- **PT3-1/2/3/4 are one problem**: the map is a diagram of a region, not a place. A
  place needs a story (what the Reclaimer is and why you cross the yards), landmarks,
  and the threat drawn as a thing, not a red rectangle.
