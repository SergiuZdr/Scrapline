## What the answers were
- **PT8-1**: the beam flies to its range plus 2 hexes and no further, at half damage past its
  range (the Lancer took 1: half of 4, less its plate). 024 let a piercing weapon be AIMED that
  far, which is how the aim got there, but the second robot stood beyond the beam's end.
- **PT8-3**: 024 let crowded labels move up as well as down; with nothing to stop it, a tag
  climbed a whole screen to the banner. Its hexes' hatching (transparent, drawn after the tag)
  was what covered HP elsewhere (PT8-4).
- **PT8-7/8**: the salvage rarity weights (60/30/10) never changed with the act; the Pour and
  the Core were tuned only by the bot; defend caches were rolled anywhere in the player's half.
