## Answers (from the code)
- PT11-2: in the run, a DEFEND fight lost with the crew alive gives no salvage and the run goes on
  (`RunSim._fight`). The bot does not plan around that: it fights to keep the caches (CombatBot
  scores shielding a cache's line), never deliberately gives them up.
- PT11-10: -7 = the Pulse Emitter's 4 + the fuel drum's blast of 3 (a blast ignores armour). Not
  wrong; the preview gave the total only.
- PT11-11: rubble and ridges cost 2 movement to enter (same hex count, longer route); rubble takes 1
  off shots at what stands in it, a ridge gives +1 range. They matter; the path takes the cheaper
  route, and among equally cheap ones the one over more scrap.
- PT11-14: the Pulse Emitter's arc (chain) does 1 less than its beam: 3 when the arc reached the
  Hauler, 4 when the beam itself did (pierce through the Pour). Not wrong; not said.
- PT11-4: "Line" is the frame's ROLE; every Line frame moves after attacking -- the note repeats
  the role's trait, which is why every Line machine carries it.
