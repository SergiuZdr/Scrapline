## What PT6-2 was
A line that runs along hex edges has two equally short paths. `CombatSim.best_line` kept the
one that reached the aimed hex, and on a tie always the same side -- and it never looked PAST
the aimed hex. A piercing beam flies on past its target, so it could plough through a crate
wall on one side with the other side open. In the screenshot the preview even said so: "a
crate wall breaks". Fixed in 016: both sides are played out and the one that does more for the
shooter is fired, then the one through fewer obstacles.
