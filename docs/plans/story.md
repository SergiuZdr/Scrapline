# Plan — Story and world

**Status:** written in 008 (2026-09-25) after play-test 3 asked for lore, a main mission,
and a Reclaimer with a purpose and a look. Delivery stays **light** (decided 2026-09-24):
text on the briefing, the map, the sites and the endings; the rest is told by what the
world looks like. No cutscenes. The words live in `data/run/story.json`.

## The premise, in one breath

Forty years ago the Meridian Combine abandoned the valley and left one machine running:
**the Reclaimer**, a harvester the size of a district, with one order — *reclaim all
material*. It is still obeying. It crawls across the yards stripping every scrap of metal,
hauls it to **the Crucible** at the head of the valley, and melts it into ingots no one
will ever collect. You have stolen the one thing that can stop it — the Combine's
**shutdown key** — and it has noticed. It follows the key. Your crew has to carry it
through three yards to the Crucible before it catches you.

## Why each piece of the game is what it is

| Game thing | What it is in the world |
|---|---|
| The front | The Reclaimer itself: a slow tide of crane booms, magnet arms and shredder mouths. It follows the key, which is why it never stops coming |
| Reclaimed zones | Stripped to bare red earth. Nothing to salvage, and the dust eats machines that linger (the HP cost of moving out of it) |
| The crew | **Free machines**: rigs the Reclaimer missed, rebuilt by the Wright (you, never seen). Three is all one mechanic can keep running |
| Enemy machines | **Spiked rigs**: machines the Reclaimer caught and fitted with a control spike. Built from the same parts as yours — which is why their parts are yours to salvage. Their red eyes are the spike |
| Enemy kinds | The Reclaimer's own drones: **trackers** (spotters), **bombers** (volatile scrap haulers), **wardens** (shield rigs), **hives** (field fabricators that build drones on the spot) |
| Scrap piles | What a machine is when it stops. Scrap buys levels: the Wright rebuilds a machine heavier each time |
| Workshops | Combine service halls with the lights still on |
| Scrapyards | Heaps the Reclaimer has not reached yet |
| The gate | Each yard ends at a Combine gate held by a Reclaimer warlord |

## The three yards (acts)

| Act | Yard | Gate | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Crane Yards** — container stacks, gantry cranes, rail spurs | The Sorting Gate | built |
| 2 | **The Slag Flats** — cooling lakes of slag, pipe forests | The Pour | 011 |
| 3 | **The Crucible** — the furnace-city itself | The Core | 011 |

## Voice

Terse, working, a little dry. People who fix things for a living. Never explain what the
screen already shows. No proper nouns a player must remember to follow the story except
the Reclaimer, the key and the Crucible.

## The Reclaimer's look (the map)

A wall across the whole width of the map: dark crane booms and harvester rigs, each with
a red beacon, under a dust cloud lit red from below. Behind it the ground is bare red
earth with nothing standing. It moves when the front moves, so the player SEES it take a
zone. Its colour is **Reclaimer red** — never used on the map for anything else.
